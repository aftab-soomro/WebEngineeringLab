The annotated image

This is the homepage of a news site broken into its regions. Every box is labelled with the HTML5 element that region should be written with.

What each box is and why

<header>
The strip at the very top that carries the site name. It is the introduction to the page, not a story.
<nav>
The block of major site links inside the header. A screen reader can jump straight to it as a landmark.
<main>
The one region that holds what this page is actually for. There is only ever one <main> per page, and the header, nav, aside and footer stay outside it.
<article>
Each news story. The test is that a story still makes sense if you cut it out and paste it somewhere else, so it earns <article> rather than <section> or a <div>.
<aside>
The right-hand column: most-read links, the newsletter box and the advert. It is related to the page but it is not the main content.
<footer>
The bottom strip with the secondary links and the copyright line.
